## 1. Product Vision & Philosophy

ATHENA is an AI-powered Media and Information Literacy (MIL) platform built for young digital citizens.

### The Problem
Young people encounter hundreds of viral claims daily across social feeds, messaging apps, and short-form videos. Current solutions rely on automated "black-box" fact-checkers that simply label content as "True" or "False". This creates passive reliance on external authorities rather than developing internal critical-thinking capabilities.

### ATHENA's Philosophy
> *"Don't just know what to believe. Learn how to evaluate."*

ATHENA turns fact-checking into an active, transparent educational investigation. Users learn to inspect evidence gaps, compare multi-source perspectives, trace how narratives mutate, and test their own reasoning skills.

## 2. Primary User Persona

**Maya, 19 — University Student & Active Social Media User**

- **Scenario:** Maya sees a viral post claiming *"Scientists have officially approved a new technology that eliminates all digital misinformation."*
- **Problem:** She finds it exciting but suspects it might be clickbait or hype. She doesn't want to blindly share or blindly dismiss it.
- **Journey with ATHENA:**
  1. Maya pastes the post into ATHENA.
  2. ATHENA generates a **Trust Passport** showing missing context and sensationalism metrics.
  3. Maya explores the **Perspective Explorer** to see how scientific bodies vs clickbait blogs frame the topic.
  4. Maya views the **Narrative Memory** timeline showing how a speculative paper mutated into a viral headline.
  5. Maya takes the **AI Tutor Challenge** to test her ability to spot sensationalized headlines.
  6. Maya's **Media Literacy Profile** updates, awarding her the *"UNESCO MIL Investigator"* badge.

## 3. UNESCO Alignment Matrix

| UNESCO MIL Target | ATHENA Feature | Educational Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical Evaluation** | Trust Passport | Teaches users to inspect source authority, evidence density, and missing context |
| **Plurality & Diversity** | Perspective Explorer | Demonstrates how the same claim is framed differently across academic, media, and community sources |
| **Information Dynamics** | Narrative Memory | Visualizes narrative mutation, headline manipulation, and viral amplification |
| **Media Literacy Education** | AI Tutor & Quiz | Converts analysis into active 2-minute learning challenges with detailed feedback |
| **Youth Empowerment** | Media Literacy Profile | Gamifies MIL skill growth across 5 core competencies (Source, Context, Evidence, Framing, Forensics) |

## 4. Key Differentiators

Unlike generic chatbots or RAG demos, ATHENA is:

1. **Non-Binary**: Replaces binary "True/False" verdicts with epistemologically grounded assessment ranges.
2. **Pedagogical**: Prioritizes teaching the user *why* reasoning works rather than just displaying data.
3. **Transparent**: Shows explicit evidence matrices and confidence/uncertainty notes.
4. **Pitch-Ready**: Features an instant 60–90 second deterministic demo mode optimized for live hackathon presentations.
